# Algo Consistency Index — methodology (v0.2, live since 2026-10-26)

## What it is
A daily reading of whether the market is paying the price patterns retail FX/CFD algorithms trade, computed per mechanism family (trend/breakout, mean-reversion, session-clock) from public market data, validated against live algo panels, with every test pre-registered before it ran and every result, including failures, published here.

## Three layers
1. **Probe index (public backbone).** Twelve frozen textbook recipes (Donchian 20/10, opening-range breakout, M15 range break, EMA 20/50 cross, RSI(2), Bollinger fade, prior-day level fade, Asian-range fade, fixed-time London entry, post-fix drift, pre-rollover fade, random-entry control) replayed on raw ticks of seventeen symbols with the broker's published spread, the raw-feed spread excess around rollover and $4 per lot round-turn commission. Reading per probe and symbol: z-score over the trailing two years of the 20-day R per trade minus the random control's R per trade in the same exit geometry. Family index = mean of its legs. Hypothetical, cost-included, never advertised as performance.
2. **Scoreboard (coincident).** Breadth, momentum z, realised drawdown load and SPRT kill count across live production accounts, labelled lagging.
3. **Pattern Integrity (leading candidates).** Fifteen standardised market-state features and a Regime Drift distance per family; descriptive. The nowcast and the per-family sizing multiplier that were to be built on this layer were dropped from the product on 2026-10-28 (change log below); no sizing instruction is published.

The T6 result means that v0.2 stays as published: no re-tuning of the readings to the live record survived an out-of-sample check, the live trend family remains the only panel the trend reading demonstrably tracks, and the mean-reversion family was audited the same day (change log below): its reading is kept as v0.2 and labelled as not representing the library mean-reversion family. No sizing rule is derived from the readings (T2, T5 and T6 together: no forecast value, no overlay value, no return persistence to size on). The sizing multiplier was dropped from the product on 2026-10-28 (change log below).

The T5 result means that the published percentile and drift alert are descriptive: used as naive position-sizing or halt rules on historical trade lists they added nothing measurable, so no sizing or halt guidance is derived from them.

## Rules that apply to every number here
Pre-registration before any test; a shift-control twin beside every statistic; day-block statistics; realised drawdown only; costs as published; probe results labelled hypothetical; negatives published; backfill drawn differently from the live record; methodology versioned with 30-day notice and 90-day overlap for major changes.

## Change log — benchmark-relative card (decision 2026-10-29, effective with the 2026-10-29 print)
From the 2026-10-29 print the page carries a sixth card, benchmark-relative performance, defined in the section of that name (added to this page with that print) and proposed by the T6 report as the one use of the benchmark that needs no new signal. Per family it shows the probe reading z, the number of live production accounts scored, the median 20-day account z, the median alpha20 (the account's 20-day z minus the family reading z, with beta fixed at 1.0 and never fitted), the alpha20 quartiles, and the share of accounts above their benchmark. It is a descriptive comparison: not a reading, a percentile, a Scoreboard statistic or an alert; nothing is re-fitted; no forecast, overlay or sizing value is claimed from it (T2, T5, T6). Aggregates only; no account login or strategy name is published. The card's date is the earlier of the last probe reading and the last Scoreboard day, so it reads 2026-10-01 until the tick data is refreshed. It is computed on the corrected registry (membership correction below): the six T&D19L13 "Agg" accounts that correction moved from mean-reversion to session-clock are scored under session-clock, and no other account's figure changed. The JSON carries it as `benchmark_relative`; every other output is computed without it. This adds an output and changes no published number, so it takes effect at once rather than under the 30-day notice that applies to changes of an existing output.

## Change log — sizing multiplier dropped (decision 2026-10-28, effective with the 2026-10-28 print)
The roadmap carried a third output, a per-family sizing multiplier (0.5–1.5, banded, with a dwell), to be published once a nowcast test (T2) and a sizing test (T3) had passed. It was never published. Four pre-registered tests have now returned the same answer from different directions: T2 found no out-of-sample forecast value in the market-state features; T5 found that overlays on the raw readings do not beat same-window permutation nulls on 105 strategy curves and 150 live accounts; T6B found no return persistence at 5–20 days in the family panels once account composition is controlled; T6C found the best of 38 sizing rules per family inside a search-aware permutation null in every window. The operator decided on 2026-10-28 to drop the multiplier from the product rather than keep it on the roadmap. From this print the index publishes no sizing instruction; the JSON field `multipliers` states that it is dropped and can no longer publish; T3 is retired and T2's record stands. The only risk outputs are the Scoreboard's realised drawdown load and SPRT kill lines, which are coincident, account-level and lagging by construction. The probe index, the Scoreboard and the Pattern Integrity features are unchanged. Should a future pre-registered test establish a sizing mechanism, it would enter as a new output under the 30-day notice and 90-day overlap rule, not by reviving this one.

## Change log — mean-reversion family audit (2026-10-28, caveat effective with the 2026-10-28 print)
The T6 finding that the mean-reversion reading moves opposite to the library mean-reversion strategies was audited the same day under a pre-registration (`aci/phase4/mr_audit`). Decomposing the published reading shows that the mean-reversion probes' own per-trade results track neither the library mean-reversion pool nor the live mean-reversion panel (rho −0.09 to +0.08); the random control they are read against does track the library pool (+0.20 to +0.38), so the published difference carried the control's cost-regime signal with the sign flipped. Re-walking the same entries with fixed 3-, 6- and 12-hour exits (one tick walk per geometry, random control matched) did not reach the pre-registered bar either: best tracking of the library pool +0.26 on 2025–26 and +0.15 on the K-cycle lockbox against a bar of +0.40. By the pre-registered rules neither hypothesis is confirmed, so the mean-reversion reading stays v0.2 and is published with the caveat "does not represent the library mean-reversion family; descriptive"; the family is not split; the next look is the 2027 live record. A third, descriptive step (splits of the same record) found the opposite movement concentrated in the K-cycle library strategies and in the legs of the fourteen non-K-cycle probe symbols, and not distinguishable from zero against the other library strategies or against the live mean-reversion accounts once account composition is removed; it changes nothing above. Nothing was chosen by result and no sign is flipped. The trend and session-clock readings are unaffected.

## Change log — publication time (2026-10-28, effective with the 2026-10-29 print)
The first 18:30 ET print (2026-10-28) read the Scoreboard through 2026-10-27. The Bachelier daily run that completes the account panel lands at about 21:15 ET, not 17:17 ET as the notice below assumed, so an 18:30 print sees a partial day and the completeness guard drops it. From the 2026-10-29 print the job runs at 21:45 ET, after that run, so a print dated D reads the Scoreboard through day D as the notice intended. The probe readings, the market-state features and the Scoreboard computation are unchanged; the 2026-10-28 print stands as published and its JSON states `scoreboard_through` 2026-10-27. Each print's JSON carries `readings_through` and `scoreboard_through`; a reader should trust those fields over the clock.

## Change log — membership correction (2026-10-28, effective with the 2026-10-29 print)
The account-family map tagged every account whose name contains "Agg" as mean-reversion before checking for an engine name. Nineteen Bachelier accounts named `T&D19Lx … Agg` or `PropMaster Agg` (33,766 trades, about half of the live mean-reversion trades) are time-of-day (session-clock) engine variants with an aggressive risk preset, and are moved from the mean-reversion to the session-clock Scoreboard. The rule order is corrected (engine names take precedence over risk-preset words); no reading, percentile, alert or statistic definition changes. The live mean-reversion and session-clock columns of the T1 and T6 rows above were computed under the previous tags; the audit record (`aci/phase4/mr_audit`) reports both. Confirmed by the operator on 2026-10-28: "Agg" denotes the engine's aggressive risk preset (25–30% drawdown allowed), not a different mechanism; the correction stands.

## Change notice (posted 2026-10-27)
Decisions taken on 2026-10-27, published here before they take effect, under the 30-day notice and 90-day overlap rule:
- **Scoreboard membership (effective with the 2026-10-27 print):** every Bachelier account with a family tag and at least ten trading days counts as production; the deploy date is the account's first appearance on the Bachelier dashboard. Before this date the Scoreboard read a validated subset (tier P1); the JSON carries the tiers used. An account is active from its first appearance to its last trade plus ten business days, and the Scoreboard reads only days on which the panel is complete (a trailing day with a handful of accounts is dropped until the full daily run lands).
- **Fourth family, carry/overnight (CO), methodology v0.3:** its pre-registration will be published before any CO reading is computed; the three-family index continues to be printed for 90 days alongside the four-family one. Not before 2026-11-27.
- **Layer 2 universe:** the market-state features move from the 17 probe symbols to 25 symbols; the probe basket itself stays at the frozen 17. Dated when the first 25-symbol reading prints.
- **Branding:** the index is published by Prismatic Algos; the page styling changes, the numbers do not.
- **Publication time (effective with the 2026-10-28 print):** the daily print moves from 06:30 ET to 18:30 ET, after the 17:00 ET broker close and the 17:17 ET Bachelier run, so that one print covers the complete broker day and governs the whole following trading day. A print dated D now reads the Scoreboard through day D (before this date it read through D−1). The computation is unchanged. **Superseded on 2026-10-28:** the Bachelier run lands at about 21:15 ET, so the print moves again, to 21:45 ET, from the 2026-10-29 print; see the change log above.
